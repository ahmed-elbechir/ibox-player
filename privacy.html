Privacy Policy — IBOX.mr Player (iPhone and iPad)

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the IBOX.mr app for iPhone and iPad ("the App", "we"). The App is a video player for the user's own IPTV playlist. It provides no content of its own.

1. Information we collect

None. The App has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no push notifications. We do not receive or store any information about you.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your playlist: the M3U link, or the Xtream Codes server, username and password you enter. It is saved only on your device so the App can reload your playlist, and you can remove it at any time from the App's profile screen.
  • App settings: your language and similar preferences, saved only on your device.

3. Connections the App makes

The App connects only to the playlist provider you entered, to load your playlist and play its streams. Those providers are third parties that are not operated by IBOX.mr; their own privacy policies apply. Your playlist and login are never sent to IBOX.mr servers.

4. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or ad networks.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No selling or sharing of any data.

5. Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
