IBOX.mr Player — Support

For iPhone and iPad

IBOX.mr is a video player for your own IPTV playlist. It does not provide, sell or include any channels, movies, series or subscriptions. You need a playlist from a provider you are allowed to use.

Getting started

  • M3U link: open the app, tap "Use an M3U link instead", type a name for your playlist, tap Continue, then paste the link your provider gave you.
  • Xtream Codes: type a name, then enter the server address, username and password from your provider.
  • Your playlist loads on your device and is sorted into Channels, Movies and Series.

Common questions

  • "Couldn't load this playlist": check the link or the server, username and password, and that your subscription with your provider is active.
  • Change or update my playlist: open Profile, then Reload playlist or Remove playlist.
  • Language: Profile → Language (Arabic, English, French).
  • Where do I get a playlist? From your TV provider. IBOX.mr does not sell or supply playlists.
